# Code of conduct

CUNEUS welcomes everyone who wants to improve it, whatever their background, level of
expertise or field. The project itself was started by a non-specialist, so questions that
seem basic are welcome.

**Expected:** be respectful and specific; criticise ideas, models and code, not people;
assume good faith; say what you know and how sure you are; credit other people's work.

**Not accepted:** harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, discriminatory
language, publishing someone's private information, or sustained disruption of discussions.

**Reporting:** contact the maintainer, H. Guettal, through a GitHub issue marked
"conduct" or privately through the contact on his GitHub profile. Reports are handled
confidentially. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, and block contributors who do
not follow this code.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
